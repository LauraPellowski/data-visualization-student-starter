Animated horizontal bar chart of MBTA average weekday ridership by line.

Play advances continuously, blending each line’s ridership and rank from one month into the next instead of holding on a single month. Commuter rail is purple and bus is yellow; other lines keep a categorical color.

Every line stays on the chart in every month, even when its ridership is 0 or missing. The play controls sit lower so they don't overlap the x-axis.
